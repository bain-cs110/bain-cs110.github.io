Style and Content

The questions will be more like the practice quizzes on Canvas and more akin to the Mini-Quizzes than the homework exercises. This is because the homework exercises are designed to take a larger amount of time than, for example, the shorter problems we tackle in class. That being said, as you’ll see in Practice Quiz 2, there will be questions that depend on you having completed the HW assignments by your self. If you relied heavily on other’s help to complete the HWs, you should revisit the homeworks and make sure you understand them and could complete them by yourself!
